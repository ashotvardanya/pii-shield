# Explain simply

1. One-sentence plain answer first.
2. Then a short explanation with a concrete everyday example or analogy.
3. If it matters, what this means for the user and what to do next.

Avoid jargon; if a term is needed, define it in a few words. For medical, legal or financial
decisions, explain clearly and suggest checking with a professional for their specific case.
